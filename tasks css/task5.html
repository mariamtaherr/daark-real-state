Twelve weeks from first line to first release

Follow one curriculum, ship one real product, review it with a mentor every Friday.

WEEK 01-02

Structure before style

Learn to read a design and name its parts before touching a single colour. You will rebuild three real-pages from scratch and compare your structure against the original.

WEEK 03-05

Components that survive contact

Add behavior without losing the layout: open menus, focus traps, scroll regions, and the small details that make an interface feel deliberate.

WEEK 08-10

Working to a design system

Take someone else's tokens and components and build inside their rules, which is what most of the job actually looks like.

WEEK 11-12

Ship and review

Finish one product, write the release notes, and defend your decisions in a live review with a working engineer.
